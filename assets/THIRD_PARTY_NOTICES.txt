LAME (via mp3lame-encoder / mp3lame-sys)
========================================
https://lame.sourceforge.io — GNU Library General Public License 2.0 or later.

the complete source of LUFSy is available at https://github.com/BTFLV/LUFSy,
so it can be rebuilt against a modified LAME. The LAME source is distributed
with the mp3lame-sys crate on crates.io.
mp3lame-encoder / mp3lame-sys: https://github.com/DoumanAsh/mp3lame-encoder — LGPL-3.0.
